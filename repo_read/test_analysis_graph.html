IR Sensor Distance

time_lapse on x-axis, distance (mm) on y-axis

far_stab (far threshold) near_stab (near threshold) clk_turn (clockwise) aclk_turn (counter-clockwise) empty (no straw)
Missing values are filled by a per-point mean over the previous 30 samples (or next 30 at the start). Raw values above 2000 are treated as missing before conversion. Empty series is generated in JS as a seeded random distance line within the observed range.
